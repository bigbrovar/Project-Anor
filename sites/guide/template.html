Tick your appliances and set a budget. The planner matches them to one of four worked designs, based on my current install. The smallest is a 3.3 kW system for lights, fans, a TV and a fridge. The largest is a 16 kW system that runs a full house with air conditioners. Each design has its own dashboard with the wiring, parts, settings and sizing checks. The limits are conservative on purpose: an efficient load is cheaper than generating more power.

Not sure what your appliances draw? How to do a home energy audit shows how to add it up first.
