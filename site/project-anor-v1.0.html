The install

A DIY install, planned, built and wired by Bigbrovar. The Multi RS Solar and MultiPlus-II share a steel rack with the two SmartSolar chargers above them and the breaker and distribution boxes below. The four battery packs stand stacked on the floor beside the rack.

The layout at a glance

Two AC islands

Light loads sit on the Multi RS. Air conditioners, the borehole pump, cooking and water heating sit on the MultiPlus-II. Each side has its own breaker, surge protector and RCBO board.

Two ways in for solar

13 panels go straight into the Multi RS's two built-in trackers at high voltage. The other 18 go through two SmartSolar chargers, one 250 V and one 150 V, straight into the batteries.

One shared battery bank

Four 14.7 kWh packs are joined on one set of copper bars, called a busbar. Every charger and inverter connects to those bars through its own DC breaker. A SmartShunt and a small home-built monitoring computer (the GX) keep track of the whole bank.
